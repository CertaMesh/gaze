# #3025 slice A scorecard: passport + national_id extension (`cfb3aed` vs `def702a`)

This report measures slice A (todo #3025) — the new `passport.cue_anchored`
recognizer and the extended `national_id.cue_anchored` — at commit `cfb3aed`
against `main` at `def702a`, the merge of slice G (#450). A is the third #3025
slice and rebases onto G, so G's shared connector (and U's resolver rung) are in
the base. It is evidence only: no accepted baseline was installed or modified,
and `--accept-baseline` was not used.

## Headline

**On the shipped default (`full-stack-kiji-resolve`) leaked labelled bytes fall
28,659 → 25,179 (−3,480, leak rate 21.9970% → 19.3255%), fully covered entities
10,291 → 10,650 (+359), zero-leak documents up. Three labels carry it:
PASSPORTID −1,810, NATIONALID −1,027, IDCARDNUM −586** (plus BUILDINGNUM −26,
incidental — a gov-ID token covering a house-number byte). Predicted from the
inventory: PASSPORTID −1,791, NATIONALID −1,017, IDCARDNUM −577 (≈ −3,385);
measured slightly more. Two-run determinism on the candidate is exact.

**False-positive ratchet — the gold-noise exception, one enumerated span.** The
deterministic cells (rule floor, pass2) rise by **+12 false-positive bytes and
+1 false-positive document**, and that is **exactly one span**: `650123456789`,
a bare 12-digit number that the corpus text explicitly introduces as *"die
Person mit der National-ID 650123456789"* and that the gold annotation does not
label. `national_id.cue_anchored` now tokenises it as `custom:national_id`. It is
a genuine national identifier the gold missed — the same gold-noise exception the
README ratchet row documents (zero A4 movement, one enumerated real-identifier
span, reviewer re-confirms). **The A4 negative corpus does not move in any
category or cell**, and no other deterministic-cell false positive appears
(rule-floor FP census: +1 span / +12 bytes, −0). This is the whole of the
deterministic-cell ratchet movement.
