# v0.12 Government-ID Scorecard

This report measures the corpus-informed government-ID cluster (`ssn.de_cue`,
`tax_number.cue_anchored`, `driver_license.cue_anchored`,
`national_id.cue_anchored`) landed by PR #427 on top of the mixed
locale-basis model, against the current `main` tip. It is evidence only: no
accepted baseline was installed or modified, and `--accept-baseline` was not
used.

### Coverage-loss disclosure

The pairwise `compare_scorecards(candidate, base)` result is
`regression.passed = false` with six failures. Its `class_coverage_loss` gate
fires three times, all in the Kiji cell and none in a deterministic cell:

| Cell | Label | Fully covered | Overlapped | Leaked UTF-8 bytes |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `full-stack-kiji-resolve` | `PASSWORD` | 6 → 7 (+1) | 99 → 97 (**−2**) | 1,739 → 1,745 (**+6**) |
| `full-stack-kiji-resolve` | `COUNTRY` | 358 → 357 (**−1**) | 364 → 364 (0) | 47 → 49 (**+2**) |
| `full-stack-kiji-resolve` | `ZIP` | 356 → 355 (**−1**) | 393 → 396 (+3) | 3,570 → 3,567 (−3) |

The `PASSWORD` loss is the credential-class Kiji `resolve` interaction tracked
as todo #2491 (mechanism #2420): the safety net operates on already-tokenized
output, so adding upstream tokens changes what it predicts; it is not a
resolver decision and collision precedence cannot touch it. On this basis the
effect is much smaller than the pre-#414 measurement (SECURITYTOKEN +32,
PASSWORD +31, USERNAME +27 leaked bytes): `SECURITYTOKEN` is flat
(1,179 → 1,179), `USERNAME` improves by 2 bytes, and `PASSWORD` loses 6 bytes
on two overlapped entities. The `COUNTRY` and `ZIP` single-entity losses are
Kiji-only collateral of the same kind. All three are trust-axis findings and
are stated here rather than hidden by the aggregate.

The remaining pairwise regression failures are the deterministic false-positive
ratchets: `false_positive_utf8_bytes` +9 in each deterministic cell and
`documents_with_false_positives` +1 in `rule-floor-extended`. A4 negative
documents remain unaffected by construction (all four recognizers replay to
0 A4 documents / 0 spans).
